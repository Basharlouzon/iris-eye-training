Iris — Eye Rest & Training

Your eyes deserve a better workday. Iris lives in your menu bar as a small
animated eye that watches your rhythm and tells you when to rest — before
your eyes complain.

BREAKS THAT RESPECT YOU
Iris follows the 20-20-20 rule (or your own custom rhythm). When a break is
due, the eye half-closes and pulses. Full-screen breaks dim everything for
your rest seconds, with a live countdown.

GUIDED EYE TRAINING
Twelve animated drills — figure eights, sweeps, spirals, near-and-far
focusing and more — drawn by a follow-the-dot trainer. Run the 4-step daily
routine in full-screen Focus Mode with music controls, or play drills
individually.

LIVING IN THE NOTCH
On notched MacBooks, Iris attaches a quiet status pill to the notch with a
live countdown, progress ring and alert badge. Hover to open the dashboard.

PLUS
• Live weather and moon phase
• Apple Music transport controls (with your permission)
• Weekly activity chart built only from what you actually complete
• Launch at login, gentle sounds, everything on-device

No account. No tracking. No data leaves your Mac.
