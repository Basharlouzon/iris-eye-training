# Iris — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 31, 2026_

Iris is a menu-bar eye-care app for macOS. It is built to be private by design.

## What Iris does NOT do

- Iris does **not** collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
- Iris contains **no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no account system**.
- Iris does not read your files, contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

## What stays on your Mac

- Your break schedule, exercise history, and settings are stored locally in your
  user defaults and are never uploaded anywhere.
- Eye-exercise progress is stored on-device only.

## Network access (only these, only outbound)

Iris makes outbound HTTPS requests to exactly two services, for one feature:

- **Open-Meteo** (`api.open-meteo.com`, `geocoding-api.open-meteo.com`) — to show
  the current temperature for the city you type in Settings. Open-Meteo receives
  only the coordinates of the city name you enter. No identifiers are sent.

If you never use the weather card, no network requests are made.

## Apple Music integration (optional, off by default)

If you enable "Music transport", Iris can show the current track and play,
pause, or skip it in Apple Music. This uses Apple's standard Automation
permission: macOS will ask for your explicit consent ("Iris wants to control
Music"), and you can revoke it anytime in System Settings → Privacy & Security
→ Automation. Iris reads only the current track's name, artist, and playback
position, and sends only play/pause/skip commands.
